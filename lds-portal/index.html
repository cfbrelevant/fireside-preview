Good questions

Clear, practical answers for newcomers and longtime members.

Can I just attend the closest building?

You're welcome to visit a meetinghouse, but the ward assigned to your residence may meet somewhere else. Several congregations can also share one building. For an assigned ward, search your full address in the official locator.

Do I need to be a member to attend?

No. Church services are open to visitors. Participation is optional, and the Church's official visitor guide explains what to expect.

How long are Sunday meetings?

Church services generally last two hours, with a sacrament meeting and a class or group meeting. Times vary by congregation; check the locator.

What about YSA or a different language?

The Church's locator can show congregations of different types, including young single adults and language congregations in some areas. Check which type fits your needs and confirm details officially.

Can I see ward boundaries or contact leaders?

Some ward maps, leader details, and member directories require signing in with a Church Account. LDS Portal doesn't collect, copy or republish private member information.

Important: This is an independent directory—not the Church's ward assignment system. Boundaries, leaders, and meeting times can change. Use the official Church locator for the latest answer.
